How to Play Infinite Craft

Welcome to Infinite Craft! This guide will help you kickstart your journey:
  • Combine Elements 🌪️🔥💧🌍

    Click on two elements to see them transform into something new. Fire and Water? That makes Steam.

  • Expand Your Creations 🌈

    Every new element is added to your collection, broadening your crafting horizon.

  • Manage Your Universe 🔄

    Use the clear and reset options to refine or restart your exploration.
